# BidLadder Project Identity

BidLadder is an open-source, bid-powered sponsored leaderboard for Cloudflare Workers.

> **BidLadder — Deploy Your Own Sponsored Leaderboard**  
> Self-hosted. MIT licensed. Deploy to Cloudflare in minutes.

## Why BidLadder

The name combines the two ideas at the heart of the project:

- **Bid:** sponsors compete for placement by bidding.
- **Ladder:** higher bids move entries higher on the leaderboard.

It is short, easy to pronounce, and lends itself to a clear visual identity. An initial name search found no closely adjacent active SaaS product using the same name. Most search noise comes from the energy-market term “bid ladder,” including the [TenneT Bid Ladder project](https://github.com/wouterbles/tennet-afrr-archive), which is in a distinct product category.

This is an initial name check, not legal clearance. A trademark search should be completed in the relevant markets before a commercial launch.

## Why the project lives under BorealBit

BidLadder is intended to be a long-lived open-source project: useful to self-hosters, welcoming to contributors, and capable of supporting a hosted service in the future. Housing it in the BorealBit organization gives the project durable stewardship and supports more granular repository roles and team permissions than a personal repository. See [GitHub’s documentation on organization repository roles](https://docs.github.com/organizations/managing-user-access-to-your-organizations-repositories/repository-roles-for-an-organization).

The ownership and public presence are intentionally complementary:

- BorealBit holds and maintains the project repository.
- Dom Liu leads development, releases, and community communication.
- The project benefits from Dom’s personal, builder-led voice.
- Documentation, infrastructure, and any future hosted offering can be operated by BorealBit.

This structure keeps the project personal and approachable while giving its code, community, and future services a stable organizational home.

## Public positioning

Use the following description for the GitHub repository:

> Open-source bid-powered sponsored leaderboard for Cloudflare Workers.

Use **“bid-powered sponsored leaderboard”** as the primary product category. Avoid relying on **“pay-to-rank”** in prominent messaging: the preferred wording makes it clear that bids purchase sponsored placement rather than manipulating search, marketplace, or App Store rankings.

## Naming alternatives considered

| Name | Strength | Limitation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **BidLadder** | Best overall fit for a memorable long-term brand | Some energy-market search noise |
| **SponsorLadder** | Most transparent description | Weaker sense of competitive bidding |
| **RankByBid** | Immediately understandable to developers | Sounds more like a feature than a brand |
| **RankAuction** | Clearly emphasizes the auction mechanism | May be mistaken for a traditional auction product |
| **BidPodium** | Strong visual and playful identity | Feels less like a developer tool |

The following names were rejected:

- **Outrank:** already associated with the established [Outrank SEO product](https://www.outrank.so/) and likely to suggest an SEO tool.
- **RankBid:** already used by the closely related [Rankbid bidding SaaS](https://www.rankbid.io/).
- **BidBoard:** widely used in procurement software and already represented by `bidboard.lol`.
- **PayRank:** can imply purchasing search or App Store ranking, creating avoidable trust and compliance concerns.

## Decision

The project is named **BidLadder** and its canonical repository is **[`borealbit/bidladder`](https://github.com/borealbit/bidladder)**. Dom Liu is the public creator and lead voice of the project; BorealBit Technology Limited is its organizational maintainer and long-term steward.
